# Tilesets Credits

I compiled these tilesets from pre-existing assets. Some minor contributions were made by me as well.

Most All Tilesets come with a Tiled Map Editor .tsx file, complete with mapping any terrain related assets that can be easily fit in, along with all map animations. Not all of them are fully complete, though.

## 4 Seasons Tilesets (LPC Revised)

The purpose of this set is to have a complete list of OGA-BY only assets. In this case, I took ElizaWy's 4-seasons and made all assets interchangeable, so you can swap the tileset out to change the season. As such, all four tilesets are identical.

I am heavily considering making a version of this that restricts it to only the tiles that change per season, so you can separate out the other sets later.

## Exterior Building Tileset (LPC Revised)

Includes all LPC Revised walls and roofs.

If you don't care about OGA-BY license, you're better off going with Bluecarrot16's lpc-walls and lpc-roofs.

One change that might be ideal is breaking down the assets into style. This mixes and matches all LPC Revised assets, but some assets don't really fit into a "medieval" setting, like the modern siding walls.

## Interior Tilesets (LPC Revised)

These are various Interior Tilesets, primarily based on LPC Revised assets. I have compiled tilesets from pre-existing assets and filled out missing gaps where needed.


### Interior Table Items Tileset

Includes all interior decorations related to table items. This includes a massive comprehensive list of all food items as well. Originally I included wall overlays, but I dropped it down to almost exclusively just table-based items, including food items.

- Authors: JaidynReiman, Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling), Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Johannes Sjölund (Wulax), BlueCarrot16, BenCreating, Durrani, YuriNikolai and Craftpix.net 2D Game Assets.
